About S. S. Graphics

A legacy built on trust & quality.

Founded in 1997, S. S. Graphics has grown from a small DTP beginning into an established printing operation with a focus on quality, customer satisfaction and continual improvement.

Our story

Printing that has grown with the people we serve.

S. S. Graphics was founded by Siddhartha Bhattacharyya and Shiv Prasad Sarmah. The business began with one computer and a single-room office, then expanded through a customer-centric approach and a commitment to dependable service.

Today, the company combines printing infrastructure with practical expertise across pre-press, production and post-production, serving commercial, corporate, publishing and everyday print requirements.
